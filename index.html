Training House
Look round: drag a finger on the view. Two fingers pinch to zoom.
Walk: the slider at the bottom left. Push it up to walk forward, down to walk back, all the way to run.
Eye height: the slider above it. Top is standing, bottom is crawling.
Tap a unit across the room and you are taken in front of it. Tap it again to work its parts.
Tap a part: it opens, comes off, cuts open or works. The card along the top lists everything else it can do.
The ? over a unit you have worked with: what it is, how it works, what we check.
Toolbox, bottom right: the multimeter, the clamp meter and the pliers.
The crawl: take the door off at the back of the house, or lift the hatch in the master closet floor, then slide down to crawl. The attic: pull the ladder down in the garage and tap it.
Drag on the view to look round. Let go and the mouse is free.
W A S D or arrows: walk. Shift: run.
C: crouch. C again: crawl (under the house). C again: stand. Half crouch: valve height.
Double click a part, or View it on the panel: it fills the view at its real proportions. Esc or a click puts it back.
A ? appears over a unit once you work with it: what it is, how it works, what we check.
Ride the drain as a ball on a sink, toilet, tub or drain pipe: W rolls down, S rolls back, Shift faster, Esc out. Drag looks round inside the pipe and double click says where you are.
Take it apart on a faucet, a shower valve or a unit: every part comes out in service order and hangs in the air with its name. Put it together puts them back.
Click a unit from across the room: you are taken in front of it, square on, at the right height. Click it again to work its parts. Walking lets go.
Click a part: it opens, comes off, cuts open or works. The panel on the right lists everything else it can do: run it, take it out, cut it, elevation.
E: elevation of what you are looking at. E or Esc: back.
Multimeter: click where the red lead goes, then where the black one goes, and it reads what is between them. Clamp meter: click a conductor. Clamp the whole cable and it reads zero, the way it really does.
Fly on: Space up, C down.
The attic: pull the ladder down in the garage and click it. The crawl: click the door in the foundation at the back of the house, it comes off, crouch to get in, then C again to crawl under the joists. Or lift the hatch lid in the master closet floor.
